How one episode is made

Eleven steps, every Tuesday

1

2

The alarm clock

A small always-on computer at Fly.io has a timer. At 5:00 in the morning, Pacific time, every Tuesday, it wakes up and starts the job.

Fly machine crontab → scripts/weekly_run.py
Tuesday, 5:00 am PacificIt adjusts for daylight saving on its own. If the week had no changelog entries, it goes back to sleep. It also looks back four weeks, in case an entry was posted late.
3

Gather the week

It downloads the changelog and keeps only that week's entries. Those entries are the only facts the episode is allowed to use.

our script scripts/backfill_plan.py

Sep 15India Endpoint Now Generally Available
Sep 17Nova-3 Pharma: Speech-to-Text for Pharmaceutical Use Cases
4

Write the script

Claude writes the middle of the show: what changed, why a developer cares, and which docs page to read. The opening and closing are fixed words, so anything about money or how to get help never comes from AI.

Claude scripts/write_episode.py · docs/show-format.md
Intro fixed words
Segments, in running order Claude
Show notes with docs links Claude
Outro: cost, credits, contact fixed words
  • Only the seven known segments, in order
  • Every link must appear in the changelog
  • No web addresses read out loud
5

Hand out the parts

Six voices share the show. Brooke hosts, and each product topic always goes to the same voice, so you learn who covers what. They pass the mic by name.

6

Speak it

Each paragraph goes to Deepgram's Flux text-to-speech in its speaker's voice and comes back as a short audio clip. A few tricky words get a spelling hint first, so "changelog" is sent as "change log".

Flux TTS scripts/render_episode.py
7

Listen back

Deepgram's speech-to-text listens to every clip and writes down what it heard. If the words don't match the script, the clip is recorded again, up to three times, and the best take wins.

Deepgram STT scripts/readback_check.py
ScriptThe real field is called TTL seconds.
HeardThe real field is called ttr seconds.
mismatch → retake 1 → heard "TTL" → keep

Across 139 checked episodes, it hears about 1 word in 340 differently from the script.

Keep a receipt

Every run saves a folder of what happened: a timed log, what the site looked like before and after, and whether it worked. If a Tuesday ever goes wrong, the proof is already written down.

Where each piece lives

One machine in the middle, three services around it

Everything that runs lives on a single Fly machine. It calls out to Deepgram and Anthropic for voices, listening, and writing, and serves the finished show to anyone who asks.
